Import & Export Resources

Learn the basics of international trade and discover useful information for importing and exporting products between Kenya, Africa and global markets.

1. How to Start an Import Business

  1. Identify products with market demand.
  2. Research reliable international suppliers.
  3. Compare supplier prices and shipping costs.
  4. Confirm import permits and regulatory requirements.
  5. Calculate the complete landed cost.
  6. Arrange shipping and customs clearance.
  7. Plan distribution and sales.

2. Essential Import Documents

  • Commercial invoice.
  • Packing list.
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill.
  • Import Declaration Form, where applicable.
  • Certificate of Origin, where required.
  • Certificate of Conformity, where applicable.
  • Relevant import permits and licences.
  • Customs declaration documents.

3. Customs Clearance

Customs clearance involves submitting the required documentation, correctly classifying goods, assessing applicable taxes and duties and obtaining customs release. Importers can engage licensed customs agents to assist with the process.

4. Exporting Goods from Kenya

  1. Identify a suitable export market.
  2. Research destination-country requirements.
  3. Identify buyers and agree on commercial terms.
  4. Prepare invoices and packing documentation.
  5. Obtain applicable export permits and certificates.
  6. Arrange transport, insurance and customs procedures.

5. Understanding Landed Costs

Landed cost is the total cost of getting imported goods to their intended destination.

  • Purchase price.
  • International freight.
  • Insurance.
  • Customs duties.
  • Applicable taxes and levies.
  • Clearing and handling charges.
  • Local transportation.

6. Avoiding Import Business Risks

  • Verify suppliers before making payments.
  • Request samples where practical.
  • Confirm product specifications.
  • Understand international payment terms.
  • Check shipping and insurance arrangements.
  • Confirm regulatory requirements before shipment.

7. Useful Official Trade Resources

  • Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)
  • KenTrade
  • Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS)
  • KEPHIS

MosesDigital Tip: Always verify applicable regulations, product classification and taxes before committing to an international purchase.

Small Business Management

Practical information to help entrepreneurs establish, manage and grow sustainable businesses.

1. Business Planning

A business plan provides direction for your business. Identify your products, target customers, competitors, operating costs, marketing strategy and financial goals.

2. Financial Record Keeping

Maintain accurate records of all business income, expenses, purchases, sales and payments. Good records help you understand business performance and prepare for tax obligations.

3. Managing Cash Flow

Cash flow management involves monitoring money entering and leaving your business. Plan payments carefully, control unnecessary spending and maintain adequate operating funds.

4. Pricing Your Products

Consider purchase costs, transportation, operating expenses, taxes and desired profit when setting prices. Review your prices regularly as costs and market conditions change.

5. Customer Service

Respond promptly to customer enquiries, communicate honestly, deliver on promises and address complaints professionally. Good customer service encourages repeat business.

6. Growing Your Business

  • Understand your target market.
  • Build a reliable customer base.
  • Promote your products online.
  • Monitor business performance.
  • Reinvest carefully in business growth.
  • Explore new markets and opportunities.

Productivity Resources

Improve your time management, organisation and efficiency with practical productivity techniques.

1. Daily Planning

Write down your most important tasks each day. Prioritise activities according to urgency and importance.

2. Time Management

Allocate specific periods for important activities. Avoid unnecessary interruptions and review your progress regularly.

3. Digital Organisation

Organise business documents, invoices, customer information and important files using clearly labelled folders.

4. Useful Free Digital Tools

  • Canva – Design and marketing materials.
  • Google Docs – Online document creation.
  • Google Sheets – Spreadsheets and record keeping.
  • Google Calendar – Scheduling and planning.

5. Working More Efficiently

  • Set realistic daily goals.
  • Break large tasks into smaller activities.
  • Reduce distractions.
  • Review completed tasks.
  • Make time for learning new skills.
